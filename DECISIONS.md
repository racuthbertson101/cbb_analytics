- 2026-10-05 (Phase 2.1): The prediction log's canonical store is git: `predictions/log/YYYY/MM-DD.csv` (rows written that UTC day) plus `predictions/HEAD.json` (row count and chain-end hash). The parquet log in the release is retired, so there is one source of truth and nothing for a failed upload to destroy. Stored precision is fixed (margin and scores 2 decimals, probability 4) so CSV round trips keep the hashes stable. `verify()` fails on an edited row, missing rows or files, rows beyond HEAD, or rows without a HEAD.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 2.2): Each night logs every game in the next 7 days, skipping only a same-day re-run. Scoring uses the last prediction made before tip-off (ESPN `game_datetime`, or 16:00 UTC on the game date when missing) and records `days_before`. Replays and rehearsals of a past date stamp `made_at` at 07:30 UTC that day.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 2.4-2.8): nightly.yml is build -> deploy and build -> sync. The build job hands data/warehouse, data/backtest, shot shards and predictions/ to sync through a one-day workflow artifact (~180 MB). sync uploads to the release, prunes old versions, then commits predictions/ with a pull-rebase retry. deploy.yml and `make site` run `pipeline.nightly --force --no-ingest --no-log`. In the offseason that path still simulates the last completed season's mid-February replay date plus its final date (computed from the season, not hardcoded); in season it always simulates the run date. Rehearsal (`--rehearsal`, workflow input `rehearsal`) uses scratch copies in data/rehearsal/ that persist across nights until `--rehearsal-reset`. `pipeline.replay` is now a fresh rehearsal. uv is cached in every workflow.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 2.6): Schema-drift guards. (1) `espn.check_summary` raises SchemaError when a summary with a box score lacks a required team stat or player key; one drifted D-I game fails the night (all 632 cached 2026 summaries carry every key). (2) Validation definitions: each core box stat (fga, fta, orb, drb, tov) may be missing in at most 1% of completed D-I team-games, as may player minutes; in the live season, 99% of team-games must have player points summing to within 2 of the team total (95% for history, where 2008-13 boxes are known to be incomplete). Every season 2014-2026 passes. (3) On Mondays in season, a canary compares the last 7 days of ESPN-ingested team boxes with the hoopR release: fail below 98% agreement on points and FGA, with no verdict under 50 overlapping team-games.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 2 rehearsal finding): opening morning (live window, zero completed games) crashed the nightly at the player tables, then at the rankings export. Fixes: (1) the live refit with no results writes the preseason prior as the season's first rating snapshot, and predictions and the log use it; (2) player tables, validation, Bradley-Terry and résumé refreshes wait for the first results; (3) the site's current season switches to the live one only once results are expected (non-exhibition games scheduled before today). Until then it shows the preseason view, and if results are expected but none were ingested the run fails. The export treats a season with ratings but no results as preseason.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 3a): Game page data. No per-game box files: the page reads both teams' `teamlogs` (2009-10 on) and `playerlogs` (2016-17 on) for the season, which the Team and Player pages share. Non-D-I teams have no logs, so their side of the box shows "D-I only" and team stats come from the D-I side's log. Expected points and rebounds use the player's season-to-date per-minute rate from earlier dates only, times minutes played, with no expectation below 30 earlier minutes (definition). OT is inferred from player minutes (200 regulation, +25 per OT). Result percentile uses the model's own normal margin distribution with the tempo-dependent sigma. The upcoming-game waterfall splits the margin from the latest ratings into offense edge, defense edge and home court. Key battles, rest and head-to-head are labeled context only.
- 2026-10-05: ESPN's midnight-Eastern tip times are placeholders, shown as "time TBA". Team colors darker than relative luminance 0.06 fall back to the alternate color, then a neutral (`web/lib/color.ts`).
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 3b.2): In-game win probability = logistic on margin/sqrt(t+1), logit(calibrated pregame p) x time share, margin, OT flag, a last-two-minutes margin term and the time share. It is anchored so it equals the pregame probability at tip-off, with the anchor fading by the end of regulation. Fit walk-forward (season S trained on 2016..S-1), using only games whose play-by-play final equals the box score. Walk-forward comparison on 2022-2026: the plan's feature set (pregame spread instead of logit, no late-game term) log loss 0.3778, worst calibration bin 6.3 pp; adopted set 0.3770, 4.8 pp. Pooled out-of-sample 2017-2026: log loss 0.3745 (pregame-only 0.55, margin-only reported per season in params/ingame.json); per regulation time bucket mean gap <= 0.3 pp, ECE <= 0.9 pp; overtime ECE 2.3 pp (KNOWN_ISSUES). ESPN's own win probabilities in the feed are not used.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 3b.3-3b.4): Score timelines (`pbp_scores`, one row per scoring change, 16 MB for 2016-2026) are a warehouse table published with the release. They are derived data; raw play-by-play stays local, per the data conventions. The live season's timelines come from the ESPN summaries the nightly already caches (identical to the hoopR parse on a check game), so charts don't wait for hoopR. Game detail (WP series, 8-0+ runs, lead changes, ties, largest leads, excitement = total WP movement with its season percentile) covers 2024-25 on, only for games whose timeline ends at the box score. Per-game shot bins live inside the team shot shards (`games` key), and the game shot chart colors each spot by FG% against the team's own season. Head-to-head, rest and common opponents use only games before the one shown.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 4a.3): Team-level matchup test, pre-registered in commit ec3125e before any code ran. Six features (tempo clash, 3PA x 3PA allowed, ORB x ORB allowed, TOV x forced TOV, FT rate x FT rate allowed, rest difference), all season-to-date from earlier dates, ridge on the walk-forward residual fit on earlier seasons, 15 test seasons (2011-12 to 2025-26, 72k games). Result: every feature alone and the joint model made pooled log loss worse (about -0.00015; 4-6 of 15 seasons improved). The rule needed +0.0005 and 10 of 15. Decision: nothing adopted, so every style feature on Compare is context only. A post-hoc check without the intercept (the registered ridge re-learns the residual mean) shows the features themselves are about zero (within +/-0.00002). It is recorded in matchup_eval.json as exploratory and does not change the decision.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 4b): Compare first load is measured from Resource Timing plus gzip sizes of the shipped files. Same season (Michigan vs Duke): 2.99 MB uncompressed, of which data is 2.22 MB raw and 0.49 MB gzipped. Cross-season: 3.76 MB data raw, 0.84 MB gzipped. GitHub Pages serves gzip, so the transfer is about 1 MB including code, under the 2.5 MB target. The 1 MB ratings file stays because the as-of selector and the sparkline need every date. Profiles take weekly system snapshots dated up to the as-of date (a snapshot dated D is fit on games before D), which brings in the season-end player-driven rating. "Rest and schedule" and the as-of selector appear only in season.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 5a.1): Substitution events, the only source of lineups, exist in the play-by-play for 2024-25 (26% of games) and 2025-26 (every game). They are absent from every hoopR file before 2024-25, and ESPN's own summaries for 2018-19 and 2022-23 games have none either (checked). The plan's ">= 90% of 2016+ games" is therefore impossible. RAPM covers 2025-26 (99.1% of D-I games with valid 5-v-5 stints for 90%+ of the game; possessions within 3% of the box in 98% of games) plus the covered 2024-25 games, and the live season from ESPN, which carries substitutions. Lineups were not inferred from players appearing in events (too inaccurate to rate players on). Stints are local data (data/pbp), like raw play-by-play.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 5b.1): Impact v2 = RAPM with a box prior (player-level ridge on RAPM: box rates plus role; position dummies dropped in attempt 2), team-consistent via a minutes-proportional shift (attempt 3; an equal per-player split widened position gaps). Smell gate after 3 attempts: 2024-25 passes (position means within 2, SD 3.0, top-25 minimum 20 mpg). 2025-26 fails one test by 0.4 (centers +2.40; SD 3.38 and top-25 minimum 22 mpg pass). Raw RAPM already has centers about 1 point above guards. Decision: v2 replaces v1 everywhere, because v1 failed every smell test badly (centers +13.7, SD 7.3, bench bigs on top), but the "experimental" label stays per the rule (KNOWN_ISSUES). Seasons before 2024-25 have box-prior-only v2 impacts. Box prior out-of-fold correlation with RAPM: offense 0.46, defense 0.29.
- 2026-10-05 (Phase 5b.2, written before running): the roster preseason prior switches from v1 to v2 impacts only if, over test seasons 2012-13 to 2023-24 (2024-25 on excluded because the box prior saw 2024-25 RAPM), pooled next-season RMSE improves for both offense and defense AND each improves in at least 7 of 12 seasons. Otherwise v1 stays in the prior.
